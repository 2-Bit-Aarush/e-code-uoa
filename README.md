## 01 / ABOUT

**E Code** is the digital home of the coding community at JK Institute of Applied Physics & Technology.

Built to bring developers, learners and technology enthusiasts together, E Code provides a central platform for the club's activities, resources and community.

More than just a website, it's an attempt to build a stronger culture of learning, collaboration and building things together.
